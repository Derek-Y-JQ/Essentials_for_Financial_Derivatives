Essentials for Financial Derivatives

A modern, self-contained monograph on mathematical finance and derivatives pricing—spanning discrete trees, Itô & rough calculus, Black–Scholes, SVI & rough volatility, exotic & American options, post-LIBOR SOFR multi-curve interest rates, and XVA.
